Package - 3D Tiles (deck.gl, spike)

This page showcases the tiles-3d package: click the 3D button in the app bar to show a deck.gl 3D Tiles view over the map. The tileset URL is read from configs/demo-3d-config-tiles-3d.json, where REPLACE_ME must be replaced by the URL of a 3D Tiles 1.1 tileset.json served with CORS enabled.
